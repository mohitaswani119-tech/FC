Privacy Policy

Who we are

The Healthy Reset ("we," "us," "our") publishes free health and wellness guides and sends related email content to subscribers who opt in through our website. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you sign up for a free guide or subscribe to our email list, we collect:

  • Your name (if provided)
  • Your email address
  • General engagement data, such as whether you opened an email or clicked a link

We do not collect payment information, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal data through this site.

How we use your information

  • To deliver the free guide or resource you requested
  • To send follow-up emails related to the topic you signed up for
  • To occasionally recommend products or resources we think may be relevant, which may include affiliate links
  • To understand which content is useful, using aggregated, anonymized engagement data

Email service provider

We use a third-party email service provider (currently MailerLite) to manage our subscriber list and send emails. Your information is stored on their servers subject to their own privacy and security practices. You can review MailerLite's privacy policy on their website.

Affiliate disclosure

Some emails and pages on this site contain affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. We only recommend products we believe may genuinely be useful, and affiliate relationships do not change the price you pay.

Your choices

  • Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link — you can opt out at any time
  • You can request that we delete your data by contacting us directly
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties

Data retention

We retain your email and engagement data for as long as you remain subscribed, or until you request deletion. Unsubscribing removes you from future email sends.

Children's privacy

This site and its content are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
